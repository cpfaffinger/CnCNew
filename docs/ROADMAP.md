# Roadmap

> **Superseded as the plan on 10 October 2026** by `docs/plan/PROGRAMME.md` (four stages: port, engine, changes,
> multiplayer; milestones and status in `docs/plan/STAGE-*.md`). This document is kept as the record of the phase states
> and acceptance statements of its time; its numbers are those of their state and are not updated any more.

## 0. Reproducible starting basis

- Copy the installation unchanged and inventory it by SHA-256.
- Record the EA source code, GeneralsGameCode and GeneralsGamePatch as local references.
- Strictly separate original, installed modifications and future engine outputs.

## 1. Asset and file system

**Status: completed on 27 September 2026.**

- Read, validate and separately extract BIG archives.
- Build a virtual overlay file system with search priority true to the original.
- Materialise the overlay once as normal files with an origin and SHA-256 manifest.
- Provide runtime access exclusively through the archive-free `CnCNew.Content`.
- Catalogue INI, STR, MAP, WND, W3D, DDS/TGA, audio and video formats.
- Use asset converters only where runtime decoding is not sensible.
- Schema 2 manifest, deterministic dependency graph, atomic incremental import and complete format/reference audit are implemented; content matrix and fallbacks are in `CONTENT-PIPELINE.md`.

- **Phase 23 "Time-step invariance and 60 Hz as the default" – completed with named limits** (7 to 9 October 2026, state `f3acc2e`; `docs/TICKRATE-MIGRATION.md` §15, `docs/SIMULATION-CORE.md` §39–§43, `docs/PERFORMANCE.md` §6c, `docs/DEMO-FINDINGS.md`): the simulation runs at **60 Hz** by default (`e8632bb`; the rate remains a value of the world, 30 and 120 Hz remain supported and are checked by the rate-equivalence suite against 30 Hz). Principle: the 30 Hz step is the definition, higher rates subdivide it – the decision is made in the first tick of the original frame of an object, the 30 Hz position is reached with the last tick, observers read the frame end. The suite (4,388 measured game quantities in ten groups, tolerances fixed before the first run and never changed) went in four rounds from 479 / 625 quantities outside the tolerance (60 / 120 Hz) to 0 / 0; the retail reference match is the same game at 30, 60 and 120 Hz. Three defects at 30 Hz were fixed along the way, each with its own manifest: the lost wake request of a slow death, the factory exit, and – after the first viewing of an AI match by the project owner – the AI saving for vehicles, own target places, boarding and army composition. **The reference values stand at 60 Hz in `baselines/reference-manifest.json`** (reference match winner FactionGLA at tick 28,260 = original frame 14,130, end hash `CA05181C36C62C7A`; stress test `9EE9F730D124C3BD`; 14 of 16 AI runs decided; `WorldSnapshot` 10, replay 12, `CommandTypes.Version` 15, `MissionSnapshot` 3, `SaveGame` 3 unchanged); older hashes, victory ticks and tick numbers in this document are 30 Hz values of their state. Performance gate at 60 Hz (8 players, 30 game minutes, measured quietly): mean 0.68–0.69 ms per tick, 99 % 1.6–1.8 ms, catch-up speed 26-fold – reached; maximum 141–143 ms and allocation 8 KB per tick – missed. Besides: a spectator mode (AI against AI in a window, `docs/SPECTATOR-MODE.md`), the skirmish AI of the original as a selectable session with three difficulty levels (not the default, not selectable in the client; `docs/SKIRMISH-AI-ORIGINAL.md`), factory exits, scroll speed, mouse assignment, build keys. **Limits:** no human has seen or heard the game at 60 Hz – there was one viewing of an AI match at 30 Hz on 8 October, before the corrections that followed from it, and nothing about spectator mode, exits, scroll speed, mouse assignment or build keys is accepted; "the suite passes" does not mean that every match is identical: 2 of 16 pairings on shipped maps end at 60 Hz in a different frame, 33 of 320 matches of the survey end more than one second away from the 30 Hz match, and 120 Hz is not measured there; the equality of whole AI matches rests on a self-acquisition per original frame of the unit, whose confirmation lies with the project owner; with the 60 Hz manifests the Linux run and the determinism matrix are not measured; a savegame written at 30 Hz loads only with `--tick-rate 30`; eleven open decisions of the project owner are in `docs/MULTIPLAYER-PLAN.md` §7a. Next step: Phase 24 (`docs/handoffs/HANDOFF-PHASE24.md`).
